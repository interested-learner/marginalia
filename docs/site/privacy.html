marginalia — privacy

last updated 19 august 2026

marginalia is a notes app for things you read. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

What stays on your phone

Everything you write. Your notes, the books you add, your tags, your follow-up thoughts, your stars, the connections the app works out between notes, your voice recordings and their transcripts, and any passage you photograph off a page. All of it is stored on the device and none of it is transmitted anywhere.

  • Voice notes are transcribed on the device. Audio is not uploaded.
  • Scanned passages are read on the device. Images are not uploaded, and no photograph is retained after the text is taken from it.
  • The connections between notes are computed on the device by a language model that ships with iOS. Your notes are not sent to any server to be analysed.

The one thing that leaves the device

When you add a book by searching for it, or by scanning the barcode on the back cover, the title or ISBN you entered is sent to Open Library (operated by the Internet Archive) to look up the author and page count. That request contains the search text and nothing else — no identifier, no account, no device information beyond what any HTTPS request necessarily carries. Open Library's handling of it is governed by the Internet Archive's privacy policy.

You can skip this entirely: every book can be typed in by hand, and manual entry is always available.

Permissions

Each is requested the first time you use the feature it belongs to, never at launch, and the app works without any of them.

  • Microphone — to record a voice note.
  • Speech recognition — to transcribe that recording, on the device.
  • Camera — to read a passage off a printed page, or scan a barcode.
  • Notifications — only if you switch on the daily reminder. It is scheduled locally on your phone; nothing is sent through a server.

Your data, and getting rid of it

There is nothing for us to delete, because we never receive anything. Deleting the app removes every note with it. There is currently no sync and no backup, so if you want a copy of your notes, use export as markdown in settings — it produces a plain text document you can keep anywhere.

Children

marginalia collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If a future version sends anything else anywhere, this page will say so before that version ships, and the app's own about screen will say so too.
