marginalia — support

notes taken from books

Something not working, or not making sense? Write to supportforapplication@gmail.com and say what you were doing and what happened. If the app showed you a message, quoting it back verbatim helps more than anything else.

Things worth knowing

Where are my notes stored?

On your phone, and only there. There is no account and no server.

Is there sync or backup?

Not yet. This is the honest answer and it matters: if you lose the phone, the notes go with it. Use export as markdown in settings to keep a copy — it writes a plain text document with every book, note and thread in it, which you can save anywhere and open in any editor.

Why don't I see any connections between my notes?

The app connects notes by meaning as you write them, but it needs something to connect: a handful of notes across at least two books before anything worth showing exists. It also works in English only at present — notes written in another language will not be connected.

Why is review empty?

The daily review shows about eight of your older notes. Until you have enough of them, there is nothing to resurface, and it will say so.

Can I fix a typo in a note?

Yes. Press and hold any note and choose edit. The same long press is how you move a note to a different book, or delete it.

I said no to the microphone or camera and now I want to use it

iOS remembers that answer. Open the Settings app, find marginalia in the list, and switch the permission back on.

The app says "the library won't open"

Your notes have not been deleted — the app could not reach them this time. Quit marginalia and open it again. If it says the same thing twice, send the message printed underneath it to the address above; that text is the useful part.
